How to read these transits

A transit is an angular relationship between the ruling planet of Kosovo and another planet, measured on the ecliptic. What matters is the angle, the tolerance around it — the orb — and above all the window: the period during which the angle stays within that tolerance. Every window shown on this page is computed from real ephemerides, not estimated.

How long does a transit last?

It depends on the relative speed of the two bodies. The Moon forms and dissolves an aspect within hours; Mercury, Venus and the Sun take days; Mars weeks; Jupiter and Saturn several months. Every window on this page is calculated from real planetary positions, which is why the durations differ from one aspect to the next.

Can I use this page to invest?

No. There is no established evidence that planetary positions predict market prices, and nothing here should be treated as a signal. Prices are driven by fundamentals, liquidity and policy. This page exists as a symbolic and educational framework — treat it as a lens for reflection, never as a reason to take a position.
