What is AstroTrader Pro?

AstroTrader Pro is a personal, esoteric and financial astrology app and webapp. It calculates a free natal chart, reads your money archetype, and studies planetary cycles and their historical correlations on transit charts, with world and market analysis. It is available in 13 languages on the web and on Android. It is an educational tool, not financial advice.

Is AstroTrader Pro financial advice?

No. AstroTrader Pro is an educational tool, not financial advice. It shows how classical astrological tradition associates planets with sectors and assets and how planetary cycles have lined up with past market history. It does not give buy or sell recommendations, price targets or guarantees, and it is not a licensed investment service. The predictive value of astrology is not scientifically proven.

Who created AstroTrader Pro?

AstroTrader Pro was created by Alberto Nicola Lanzafame, an independent developer based in Palermo, Italy, who designed the astrological model, wrote the software and runs the project. The AstroTrader Pro™ trademark application was filed with the Italian Patent and Trademark Office (UIBM), application no. 2026000082570.

Who is Alberto Lanzafame?

Alberto Nicola Lanzafame is an Italian actor, trader and astrologer from Palermo, and the creator of AstroTrader Pro. He worked as a professional actor from 2008 to 2018 (credits include The Hunter, Felicia Impastato and Il Clan dei Marsigliesi; IMDb nm8412826). Since 2019 he has worked as an independent trader and market analyst, publishing algorithmic trading tools on MQL5 (alb900) and indicators on TradingView (alblanz). He has studied astrology for about 18 years across classical, Hellenistic, Vedic, Morinian and esoteric traditions, and offers personal astrology consultations.

Which markets does AstroTrader Pro analyze?

AstroTrader Pro maps 211 financial instruments to their ruling planets: 39 stock indices, 80 stocks, 37 ETFs, 23 commodities, 18 forex pairs and 14 cryptocurrencies. It also includes a world map of financial astrology by country. Each asset is read through its planetary rulership and, where known, its founding or listing chart.

Which astrological methods does it use?

Classical and modern sources: Ptolemy, Morin de Villefranche, Alice Bailey (esoteric astrology), Volguine, Carter, Ebertin and André Barbault (cyclical index). It uses the tropical zodiac, planetary dignities (domicile, exaltation, detriment, fall), houses, aspects, transits to the natal chart and lunations.

Has the method been tested on historical data?

Yes, as a study of historical correlations: about 52 years of data, 52 instruments and 180,996 observations. The results describe past associations between planetary cycles and market behaviour. They are not trading signals and do not predict future prices.

Is AstroTrader Pro free?

The natal chart calculation is free and requires no registration. Extra content, such as detailed reports and personal transits, is available with an optional Premium subscription.

Where is my birth data stored?

Birth data stays on your device. AstroTrader Pro has no advertising and no analytics tracking; the only data processed is what subscription management strictly needs. Details are in the privacy policy.

13 languages: English, Italian, Spanish, French, German, Portuguese, Dutch, Russian, Chinese, Japanese, Korean, Arabic and Hindi.
