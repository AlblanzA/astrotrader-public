Astrological profile of Morocco

Water signs describe an economy driven by liquidity, credit and collective mood. Cycles are long and tidal, defensive behaviour dominates in contraction, and confidence matters more than any single figure.

Its fixed modality means persistence: trends here last longer, accumulation phases are extended, and reversals require considerably more pressure to occur.

How to read these transits

A transit is an angular relationship between the ruling planet of Morocco and another planet, measured on the ecliptic. What matters is the angle, the tolerance around it — the orb — and above all the window: the period during which the angle stays within that tolerance. Every window shown on this page is computed from real ephemerides, not estimated.

Which zodiac sign rules Morocco?

Morocco is traditionally associated with Scorpio, a water sign of fixed modality, whose ruling planet is Mars. The attribution comes from mundane astrology — the branch that applies astrological symbolism to nations rather than to individuals.

What does Mars govern for Morocco?

Mars governs energy, industry and conflict. Applied to markets it points to oil and gas, defence, metals, heavy manufacturing — sectors that move with force and rarely gradually.

How long does a transit last?

It depends on the relative speed of the two bodies. The Moon forms and dissolves an aspect within hours; Mercury, Venus and the Sun take days; Mars weeks; Jupiter and Saturn several months. Every window on this page is calculated from real planetary positions, which is why the durations differ from one aspect to the next.

Can I use this page to invest?

No. There is no established evidence that planetary positions predict market prices, and nothing here should be treated as a signal. Prices are driven by fundamentals, liquidity and policy. This page exists as a symbolic and educational framework — treat it as a lens for reflection, never as a reason to take a position.
