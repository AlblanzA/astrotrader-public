The project

Two traditions, one tool

AstroTrader Pro unites two distinct knowledge traditions in a single coherent mobile app: the canonical Western astrology of Ptolemy, Volguine, Barbault, Bailey and Palamidessi, layered with the twentieth-century financial-astrology lineage of Gann, Bradley, Ebertin, Merriman, Meridian and Crawford. The result is an interactive zodiac wheel where every planetary transit becomes a symbolic signal mapped to 200+ financial assets across indices, stocks, commodities, forex, crypto and ETFs.

The roots

Standing on giants' shoulders

Every rulership, archetype and aspect doctrine in the app comes directly from the canonical authors of two intertwined disciplines.

Classical astrology

Two thousand years of tradition

Claudius PtolemyII ADTetrabiblos — foundational system of signs, houses, aspects and planetary natures.
Alexander Volguine1903–1976Mundane astrology and lunar cycles applied to collective events.
André Barbault1921–2019Pioneer of statistical mundane astrology and cyclical index methodology.
Alice Bailey1880–1949Esoteric Astrology: hierarchical correspondences between planets, signs and nations.
Tommaso Palamidessi1915–1983Archeosophy: esoteric archetypes of planets and sectors.
Financial astrology

A century of market application

William D. Gann1878–1955Tunnel Thru the Air (1927) — the seminal work on planetary-cycle market timing.
Donald Bradley1925–1974The Siderograph: aggregated planetary aspect index for sentiment cycles.
Reinhold Ebertin1901–1988Cosmobiology: planetary-picture methodology for bull/bear bias extraction.
Raymond Merrimancontemp.Stock Market Timing (5 vols) — modern reference for planetary market cycles.
Bill Meridiancontemp.Planetary Stock Trading — corporate natal-chart catalog and IPO analysis.
Arch Crawfordsince 1977Crawford Perspectives — longest-running financial astrology newsletter.
