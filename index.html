ASTROTRADER PRO™

Where two-thousand-year astrological tradition meets modern financial markets
AstroTrader Pro unites two distinct knowledge traditions in a single coherent mobile tool: the canonical Western astrology of Ptolemy, Volguine, Barbault, Bailey and Palamidessi, layered with the twentieth-century financial-astrology lineage of Gann, Bradley, Ebertin, Merriman, Meridian and Crawford. The result is an interactive zodiac wheel where every planetary transit becomes a symbolic signal mapped to 200+ financial assets across indices, stocks, commodities, forex, crypto and ETFs. Scroll time forward, watch the planets move, see which sectors come into bullish trine — or which fall under bearish square — at any moment from today to the next decades.

Two traditions, one tool

Classical astrological lineage

Claudius Ptolemy (II AD, Tetrabiblos) · Alexander Volguine (1903–1976, mundane astrology) · André Barbault (1921–2019, statistical cyclical indexes) · Alice Bailey (1880–1949, esoteric correspondences planet ↔ sign ↔ nation) · Tommaso Palamidessi (1915–1983, archeosophical archetypes of sectors).

Financial astrology layer

W. D. Gann (1878–1955, Tunnel Thru the Air 1927) · Donald Bradley (Siderograph index) · Reinhold Ebertin (1901–1988, cosmobiology) · Raymond Merriman (Stock Market Timing) · Bill Meridian (Planetary Stock Trading) · Arch Crawford (Crawford Perspectives, since 1977).
